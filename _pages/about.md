# About me

I am a Master's student in **Electronic Information** at the School of Physics, **Northeast Normal University**. 

My research interests lie in **computational imaging** and **optoelectronic sensing**, including:

- Single-pixel imaging (SPI) 
- Polarimetric and multispectral imaging
- Image-free visual tasks driven by deep learning
- Computer vision and embedded systems
