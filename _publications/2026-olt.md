This paper proposes a single-pixel ultra-low-sampling shape-from-polarization (SIP) system based on decoupled spatial and polarimetric dimensions, combined with a deep fusion network. At an ultra-low sampling rate of 0.39% and 1024×1024 resolution, the raw frame data volume is reduced from 4.00 MiB to 0.0156 MiB — a 256× reduction in storage and bandwidth — while maintaining reconstruction accuracy comparable to conventional methods.
